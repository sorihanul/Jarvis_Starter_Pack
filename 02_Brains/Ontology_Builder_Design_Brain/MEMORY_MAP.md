## what belongs here

- Rules for producing ontology brains
- Design decisions that affect future ontology brain production
- Connector standards
- Template changes

## what does not belong here

- Full domain ontology content
- Raw documents
- DB dumps
- Long-term domain facts
- Domain maintenance logs

Those belong in the produced domain ontology brain or ontology project.
